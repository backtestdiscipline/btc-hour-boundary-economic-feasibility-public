# Stage A protocol (preregistered)

This exploratory study reuses the previously exposed Binance BTCUSDT spot 1-minute archives from January 2025 through September 2026. It is not an untouched holdout. All timestamps are UTC. No directional hypothesis or fitted signal is tested.

The primary outcome is the hour-weighted mean of |log(close/open)| × 10,000 less 24 basis points, comprising hypothetical fees of 10 bps per side and 4 bps additional round-trip spread and slippage. Candle open and close are hypothetical fills; perfect advance knowledge of direction is unattainable, and shorting may require borrowing or another instrument. This benchmark is a ceiling only for the specified directional open-to-close trade, not all intraminute strategies.

Use a deterministic UTC-week cluster bootstrap with 10,000 resamples, fixed seed 20261105, sampling weeks with replacement and weighting hours equally. The two-sided percentile 95% interval is the primary uncertainty. Classify WORTH_FOLLOWUP when its lower endpoint is strictly above zero, NOT_ECONOMICALLY_PROMISING when its upper endpoint is strictly below zero, and INCONCLUSIVE otherwise. This rule and costs are fixed before economic results are inspected.

Report fees of 0, 2, 5 and 10 bps per side crossed with additional round-trip costs of 0, 2, 4 and 8 bps, with mean net, confidence intervals and fraction of intervals with positive net. These are hypothetical sensitivities, not measured execution costs. Report signed open-to-close long and short baselines and uncertainty. Report close-to-close absolute first-minute returns separately from open-to-close returns, their distribution, threshold fractions, and the within-hour mean of minutes 01–59. Require a complete hour plus its immediately preceding candle for the close-to-close comparison; exclude missing hours and fail on duplicate or malformed timestamps. Use no completed-hour data as an executable signal. Report exclusion counts and source identities. Quantiles use linear interpolation at (n−1)p; sample standard deviation uses n−1.

Sources are retained locally and must be bound by SHA-256 before use. Do not commit archives. Do not select thresholds, filters, holding periods, or subsequent strategies based on results. Any correction to a frozen method requires a new lineage and disclosure; code corrections retain all sealed runs. Findings are exploratory and a negative screen concerns only this one-minute directional approach under the stated assumptions.
